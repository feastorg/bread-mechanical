# Slice case and backplane part

A parametric, data-driven OpenSCAD case for BREADS Slice boards, and a printed
backplane part for the Loaf_x004 backplane PCB. The case, the backplane part and
a future stand are separate parts. They meet only through the slot interface,
`design/synth/slot-interface-v1.1.json`.

Status (2026-10): a Slice_RLHT case (`s_tray_nano-b`, `s_lid`, `panel_rlht`) and
the backplane part were printed and fitted to a real Slice_RLHT and Loaf_x004. The
bus socket, guide, ear towers and lid spigots fit as designed. The interface is
still marked provisional until the open items below are measured.

## What is here

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `design/synth/case.scad`, `panel.scad` | The case: tray (THT side), lid (SMT side), and the sliding front panel, the one per-slice part |
| `design/synth/slot-interface-v1.1.json` | The slot interface: frame, seat, bus window, tongue, guide, towers, envelope, loaf hole pattern |
| `design/synth/formats.yaml` | Board formats (S 70 x 100, L 100 x 145), MCU USB access, named exceptions. A stand-in for the bread-infra format table |
| `design/synth/resolved/` | Per-slice parameters, generated by `tools/resolve.py` from each slice's `slice.yaml` and `.kicad_pcb`, one line per section |
| `design/synth/legends/`, `modifiers/` | Panel legends (a stand-in taken from the silkscreen, until the legend source is decided) and per-slice modifiers |
| `design/synth/spec.py`, `sweep_spec.py`, `tools/` | partspec checks for the case, the all-slices sweep, the mutation sweep, and helpers |
| `design/backplane/` | The backplane part, its loose stand feet, and its checks, including a clash check against real cases in every slot |
| `design/interface/tools/` | The KiCad parsers the interface numbers come from |
| `print/` | What to print and how (`README.md`), the print-oriented sources and coupons (`src/`), and `build.sh` |
| `concepts/REQUIREMENTS.md` | The case requirements the specs cite |

## Building

Requirements: OpenSCAD with the Manifold backend (tested on 2026.10.03),
[partspec](https://github.com/heibench/partspec) 0.8.0, Python 3 with PyYAML.

STLs are not tracked. Build them all into `print/1-coupons/`, `print/2-cases/`
and `print/3-backplane/`:

```
bash print/build.sh
```

Every STL is in print orientation with its bed face at z = 0. `print/README.md`
says what each part is, the print settings, and the hardware.

## Checks

From `design/synth/`:

```
partspec check $(python3 -I tools/targets.py)   # the case: 60 parts
python3 -I tools/mutate.py                      # each mutation must fail its check
```

The all-slices sweep runs `sweep_spec.py:p_<i>`, `a_<i>` and `n_<i>` for every
file in `resolved/`. From `design/backplane/`, the backplane checks are the
targets in `spec.py`, and `bash mutate.sh <scratch dir>` is its mutation sweep.

Known failures: Slice_DCMT (a 1.85 board, below the envelope) and
Slice_TEMP_NUCL_L4L-r1 (board probe). In the sweep, Slice_IAQM's LED and the far
corner and panel size of Slice_AOEM, Slice_THMX and Slice_TEMP_NUCL_L4L-r1. Each is
a board that differs from its format. Slice_AOEM, Slice_THMX and
Slice_TEMP_NUCL_L4L-r1 each have an open issue on their repo.

To regenerate `resolved/` after a slice changes, clone the Slice_* repos side by
side and run `python3 -I tools/resolve.py <Slice_name>...`. It looks for them in
the folder that holds this repository, or in `$BREADS_REPOS`.

## Parameters

- **Interface** (`slot-interface-v1.1.json`): pitch 30.4, seat, towers, guide,
  bus window, tongue, envelope, board thickness range 1.4 to 1.8, loaf hole
  pattern. Changing one is a new interface version and a backplane reprint.
- **Print fit**: insert hole, spigot, ear hole, panel clearance (`pan_gap`) and
  detent (`bump`), rim gap, bed chamfer, legend depth. These depend on the
  printer, nozzle and material; the coupons in `print/1-coupons/` calibrate
  them.
- **Case design**: wall, roof and floor thickness, chamfers, front inset.
- **Per slice**: from each slice's own data (`resolved/`, `legends/`, `modifiers/`).

## Design decisions

- One-sided clamshell. The tray takes 4 M3 heat-set inserts (M3 x 5.7, OD 4.6,
  vendor hole 4.0); 4 M3x10 go in through the lid. Lid spigots (4.7) locate the
  board in its 5.0 holes. No nuts anywhere.
- 29.6 thick at 30.4 pitch. The case ears screw into inserts in the backplane
  part's towers (T1 -56.4, T2 +56.4, T3 +101.4 at N 11.5), each format using two.
- The backplane part sits on the loaf top, held by 8 M3x6 from the loaf underside
  into inserts at the bus-footprint 3.3 holes. It fits Loaf_x004 gen1 and r3,
  which share every slot feature and differ only in outline.
- A per-slot U guide on the backplane part locates the slice socket 6.35 before
  the pins meet. The guide, not the ear screws, sets the slice position.
- Front: a sliding panel with V tongues in the tray towers and a spring-finger
  detent, carrying an engraved legend and leaving a wire slot open at the bottom.
  Re-terminating a packed slice may mean pulling it (2 ear screws).
- MCU USB: a plug-sized port at each board's own Nano USB, tall enough for a
  soldered or socketed Nano. S trays come in two USB groups, `nano-a` and
  `nano-b` (`print/src/groups.py`).
- 2021 gen-1 slices are not supported.

## Open items

- Two cases packed at 30.4: neighbour clearance, and hex-key reach to the ear
  screws with neighbours in place.
- Socket-to-header float with the ears seated (designed 0.13).
- Re-terminating in place under a packed neighbour (`print/1-coupons/tilt_jig`).
- The legend source: a `slice.yaml` field or schematic net labels.
- A stand that fastens to the backplane part's underside, in place of the loose
  feet.
- A snap-in plug for the Nano USB window.
